HEDGE-HOGGER

A hedgehog mother has five hoglets and one night. At dusk she sees them over the lane and the canal to the orchard. Before dawn she has to bring every one of them back. Hop through the traffic, ride the boats and logs, keep clear of the fox, the heron and a farmer with a torch, and get each hoglet to the other side.

The hoglet

Seen side-on, facing right. It flips to face the way it last hopped and never rotates. If a pick faces left, press Mirror.

On the road

Five lanes, alternating direction. One touch sends the hoglet curling back to where it started, and costs a life. After dark, headlights reach ahead of every vehicle, so you can see it coming.

On the canal

Hedgehogs can't swim this far. Ride what floats, and hop off before it carries you off the screen. Logs and narrowboats are drawn in code, with random lengths and paintwork. These three are sprites.

On the land

The verges and the towpath are safe from traffic, but not from these two.

Bonuses

How a night plays

Out, then backA night is two legs. Out: get all five hoglets from the garden burrows to the orchard nests. Back: bring all five home again. Each leg is a new layout and a little harder.
One hoglet at a timeEach hoglet has 30 seconds. A hedge or a full home just bumps you back. A vehicle, the water, the fox, the heron, the farmer or the clock costs a life, and the hoglet goes back to its start.
Hop comboHop forward again within half a second of landing to build the combo: ×2, ×3, up to ×4. It multiplies hop and home points. Stopping or hopping backward resets it.
Seeded lanesEvery leg is laid out from a seed: lane speeds, gaps, boat lengths, which duck families dive. A retry repeats it, so you can learn it. The next leg is new.
LivesStart with 3. An extra life at 10,000, then every 20,000.
The rampPer leg: traffic and boats about 8% faster, gaps a little tighter, ducks dive more often, the farmer turns up sooner. The fox and the farmer join from night 2.

From the arcade original

Re-imagined from Konami's 1981 Frogger. The mechanics are borrowed. The setting, characters, art and layouts are new.

Kept
  • One-tile hops in four directions on a single screen
  • A road of alternating lanes, a safe strip, then water you cross on moving platforms
  • Five homes to fill, a timer per crossing, points for new rows and time left
  • Platforms that sink under you (turtles there, ducks here), and a bonus in a home
  • A friend to carry home for extra points
Changed
  • A hedgehog family in the English countryside instead of a frog
  • Tractors, vans and motorbikes on a country lane; narrowboats, ducks and lily pads on a canal
  • A towpath before the homes, so the last hop is about timing, not luck
  • Hitting a hedge or a full home bumps you back instead of killing you
New
  • The return trip: every night is out and back, dusk to dawn
  • A farmer who appears without warning and chases you, and can be lured into the traffic
  • The hop combo, and seeded lanes you can learn
  • A heron that stabs at lily pads, a fox on the middle verge
  • Headlights, fireflies and dawn light; controller and touch from day one
